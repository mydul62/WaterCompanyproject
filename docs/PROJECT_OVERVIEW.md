# Project Overview: Xclusive Can Limited (waterWeb)

## Executive Summary
**Xclusive Can Limited** (and sister brand **Q Pail**) is one of Bangladesh's premier industrial packaging manufacturers, operating since 1992. The company specializes in manufacturing custom-engineered tin cans, aerosol containers, food cans, and plastic (PET, HDPE, PP) bottles, pails, and closures. With dual manufacturing facilities in Gazipur and Tongi holding **LEED Platinum**, **ISO 9001:2015**, **ISO 22000**, **HACCP**, and **GMP** certifications, Xclusive Can supplies packaging to global and local FMCG, paint, pharmaceutical, and chemical brands.

## Business Objectives
1. **Brand Authority:** Establish an ultra-modern, high-tech industrial online presence reflecting 30+ years of manufacturing leadership and green manufacturing excellence.
2. **B2B Lead Generation:** Transform static browsing into high-value corporate inquiries through an interactive **Request for Quotation (RFQ)** and sample dispatch engine.
3. **Interactive Product & Specification Showcase:** Provide corporate buyers, procurement officers, and packaging engineers with instant access to technical container specifications (dimensions, neck finishes, brimful capacity, barrier properties).
4. **Editorial & Media Hub:** Highlight national media coverage, press stories of industrial leadership, and sustainability reports.
5. **Centralized Administration:** Provide non-technical client teams with a comprehensive **Sidebar-based Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)** to manage products, inquiries, press releases, and job applications.

## Key Target Audiences
- Corporate Procurement Managers (Paints, Food & Beverage, Lubricants, Pharmaceuticals, Cosmetics)
- Packaging Design Engineers seeking custom mold tooling and technical specifications
- Investors, Industrial Partners, and International Export Buyers
- Job Seekers and Technical Talent looking for manufacturing engineering careers
